iOS iPhone Calendar & Reminders Sync

One-Tap iOS Bridge

Sync your Melody calendar events (assignments, deadlines, annual friend birthdays & weekly classes) to Apple Calendar, and push your to-do reminders straight into the iOS Reminders app! 📱🎀

🗓️

1. Sync to iOS Apple Calendar

Imports directly into iPhone / iPad default Calendar

Generates an Apple-compatible .ics Calendar Bundle containing your scheduled tasks, assignment deadlines, yearly recurring friend birthdays (RRULE:YEARLY), and weekly classes.

🔔

2. Sync to iOS Reminders App

Sends pending tasks & deadlines with alerts to Reminders

Exports all pending tasks & assignment deadlines as RFC 5545 VTODO Reminders with due dates, priority flags, and 30-minute early alerts — or sends them via Apple Shortcuts.

✨ On iPhone Safari: Tap "Sync to Apple Reminders (.ics)" and select Add All in the Reminders sheet.
⚡ Shortcuts Option: Copies your formatted reminder list & launches Apple Shortcuts.
